## Part 2: Sent to human review (1)

### 9. Charpy Test Machine

| | |
|---|---|
| **Solicitation** | W8703-270067/B |
| **Tender link** | [CanadaBuys notice](https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/cb-620-44964870) · [RFP PDF](https://canadabuys.canada.ca/sites/default/files/webform/tender_notice/113318/w8703-270067b---charpy---rfp-en.pdf) |
| **Closes** | 2026-11-27 14:00 (14 days remaining) |
| **Outcome** | Lead Review, leaning undecided |
| **Reason code** | REQUIREMENT UNCONFIRMED / DEALER ADVANTAGE (decided at deep dive) |
| **Verdict / confidence** | human_review, confidence 70 |

**Sent to the lead because:** it is test equipment (a Go category) and goods supply is confirmed in the RFP. Felix's ability to supply it could not be confirmed. The knowledge base has no Charpy or QETE history for Felix and no partner source. It is evaluated on lowest price, which favours manufacturers and dealers. The value is estimated at about 60,000–100,000 CAD (tentative, unverified), close to the 50,000 CAD test-equipment floor.

**What the RFP says (read in full text)**
- One ASTM E23 Charpy machine, 5–400 J, with fixtures, anvils, supports, reference materials, software and a computer if the design needs one (Statement of Requirement 3.1.1, 3.2.4; Pricing Schedule items 1–12).
- No security requirement (s.2.1). No brand, ACAN or supply-arrangement wording found.
- Evaluated on lowest evaluated price with mandatory technical criteria (s.11.1).
- Suppliers must be Canadian or from an applicable trading partner (s.2.2). Felix meets this.

**Skill reference**
- **Line 100:** "An unconfirmed requirement means Lead Review in a Go or Lead category, and Reject otherwise."
- **Line 109:** "Lead Review in every other case…"
- **Line 101:** "do dealers or OEMs bid directly?"
- **Line 102:** "Note when lowest price on an unfamiliar product weakens the case."
- **Line 103:** "Felix history: … History supports a decision but never overrides points 2 to 6."
- **Line 61:** "For test and measurement equipment the floor is 50,000 CAD."

> **Question for the lead (skill line 7):** Does Felix have a manufacturer or partner source for a fully ASTM E23-compliant Charpy machine at a price that can win on lowest price, and is the realistic value above the 50,000 CAD floor?

**Limits:** only the English RFP PDF was listed and read (74 pages, as extracted text; no amendments listed). The memory cannot search project names or descriptions, so "no Charpy history" means not recorded, not proven absent.
